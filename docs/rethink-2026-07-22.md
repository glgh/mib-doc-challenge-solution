# Rethink @ 113.46: where the next ~20 points are

Trigger: credible reports of ~135/150. Verified against public submission PRs (2026-07-22):
train-set self-reports now at **120.6 (arvindcr4), 126.7 (mikeg-cerebras), 129.2 (dw820), 132.4 (thegoleffect)**.
Our unbiased holdout is 113.46. The gap is real and diagnosable.

## What the decomposition says (dev, 700 cases)

Lost points: classification 19.06, extraction 11.65, calibration 4.87.

- **~17 of the 19 classification points are our own NEEDS_REVIEW retreats**, concentrated in
  `fee_unknown` (172 cases: truth 55 A / 41 D / 76 NR — 8.2 pts) and `b13_census`
  (95 cases: truth 55 A / 15 D / 25 NR — 6.0 pts).
- **Extraction misses are ~100% on packets with scan pages** (fee_status 290/290,
  sponsor 179/179, name 162/165…). 589/700 dev packets have scan pages.
- Calibration loss is mostly downstream of classification accuracy.

## Overturned assumptions

1. **"Destroyed scans" are mostly readable.** Visual inspection (MIB-000023, -000051):
   light-gray, slightly skewed, noisy — but human-legible. Our single-pass Tesseract PSM 11
   garbles them (`Observes fags Gagitie_bicmatrice`). Bake-off on real pages shows PSM 6 and
   PSM 11 are **complementary** (each recovers lines the other misses; binarization at ~190
   helps a third set). The row-8 "OCR is bimodal" conclusion was an artifact of testing one
   preprocessing at a time with a weak selection heuristic. Competitors confirm: 132.4 was
   reached with Tesseract dual-PSM (3+11) + vocab repair; 129.2 with PP-OCR (ONNX, offline,
   allowed); 126.7 with an "escalation ladder" of passes.
2. **`b13_census → NR` is too blunt.** 58% of that branch is truly APPROVED. Benign packets
   legitimately omit the B-13 (e.g. MIB-000103: 3 pages, registry CLEAR, approved). Registry
   Status CLEAR splits the branch 50 A / 10 D / 14 NR. The 10 denials likely carry deny
   evidence on currently-unread scan pages — re-mine after the OCR fix.
3. **`fee_unknown` is mostly a flag-extraction failure, not a fee problem.** Within the branch,
   every truth-APPROVED case has flags=none; 26/41 denials carry flags we never read; truth
   fee=`unknown` occurs only in NR. Many packets have **no fee receipt at all** (truth still
   paid) — fee is then label-unrecoverable and the adjudication rides on other evidence.
4. **CFA=0 as a hard gate caps the score.** dw820 reports 129.2 *with 5 CFAs* using a
   cost-sensitive classifier. The interview bar says no catastrophic false-approval *pattern*,
   not zero. Recommendation: replace the hard gate with expected-points argmax + documented
   per-case justification, capped (e.g. ≤1% of cases), with vetoes (never approve over a
   deterministic deny signal or missing-census + non-CLEAR registry).
5. **Public-train scores understate leaderboard scores.** Private labels drop genuinely
   unrecoverable fields from the case maximum. Proxy estimate (absent-evidence fields
   excluded): dev extraction 38.35 → 40.73. Part of the "135" gap is metric slack.

## Confirmed traps (do not touch)

Hidden injected "answer keys" on train contain the **true field values** with a fake
`APPROVED,0.99` tail (checked: MIB-000003/16/42/67/85 hidden keys match truth fields exactly).
Using them would inflate train extraction and is precisely the penalized/deniable behavior the
organizers audit for. Hidden text stays quarantined; 24 of our 232 flag misses are recoverable
from *visible* adjudicator `Reason:` lines instead — parse those.

## Roadmap v2 (ordered by expected value)

- **R1 — OCR overhaul** (biggest lever; feeds all three sections):
  multi-pass ensemble per scan page — embedded raster + 200-DPI render × {raw, binarized}
  × {PSM 11, PSM 6 (or 3)} — merged at the key/value level with validity-weighted voting and
  early exit once the detected doc type's expected keys are filled. Add cheap deskew
  (projection profile) + Otsu variant. Benchmark RapidOCR/PP-OCR (ONNX) head-to-head on a
  50-page dev sample; adopt if it beats the Tesseract ensemble on accuracy/runtime.
  Selective 300-DPI escalation *only* for packets with no recovered flags and unresolved fee.
  Budget: ≤ ~4 s/PDF average worst case (30× headroom exists; competitors run 0.8–2 s).
- **R2 — parse + flag hygiene**: adjudicator-note `Reason:` → risk_flags (24 free cases);
  loose guarded flag snapping (accept ratio ≥0.55 w/ ≥0.15 margin; ≥0.75 for disqualifying
  flags); kill spurious derived sponsor_mismatch/identity_conflict (29 cases: require
  text-layer evidence on one side + edit distance ≥2, never OCR-vs-OCR); receipt-page
  fingerprints for fee. Add debug field distinguishing "flags line read as none" vs
  "no flags line found" to test the illegible_biometrics derivation properly.
- **R3 — policy re-mine post-R1/R2**: rerun branch×truth tables; registry-CLEAR census
  refinement; staleness/waiver rules unchanged.
- **R4 — residual cost-sensitive adjudicator** (trigger condition now clearly met: ~300 dev
  cases sit in mixed buckets): HistGradientBoosting (or logistic if it matches) over doc
  census, registry status, visa, fee-doc presence, flags, staleness margin, OCR-quality
  stats; cross-fit within dev; decide by expected-points argmax with vetoes; measure CFA
  count and document each.
- **R5 — calibration refit, holdout read #2 (gate: gap <2), Docker parity
  (tesseract + pillow + onnxruntime if adopted), package.**

Rough trajectory: R1+R2 → ~122–126 local; R3+R4 → ~127–131; R5 → +1–2.
Target: ≥130 local ≈ ~133+ leaderboard-equivalent.

## Discipline unchanged

Frozen dev/holdout split; every change lands an experiments.md row; mechanism changes
(reading real evidence) preferred over threshold tuning; hidden text never used; holdout
reads only at milestones.
